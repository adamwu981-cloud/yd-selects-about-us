Our story

Yunnan taught us how to look.

01

From commercial origin to specialty voice

A region revealing what it can become.

Coffee has grown in Yunnan since the late nineteenth century, yet for much of its modern history it was known mainly as a dependable commercial origin. That description never told the whole story.

Across Baoshan, Pu'er, Dehong, Menglian, Lincang and other mountain areas, producers planted higher, selected ripe cherries more carefully, revived varieties such as Typica and explored washed, natural, honey and controlled fermentation methods.

Their patience revealed coffees with florals, citrus, tropical fruit, tea-like clarity, warm spice and deep sweetness. These producers shaped our understanding of what Yunnan coffee can become.

Our mission

Make quality worth repeating.

One outstanding lot can capture attention for a season. Lasting quality creates lasting confidence.

We select for flavour and integrity, pay for quality in ways that give farms a reason to keep improving, and protect each lot through transport and quality control. At origin, we support experiments with varieties, field management, processing and controlled fermentation.

The goal is not to cover Yunnan's natural character with an invented flavour. It is to understand the process well enough to reveal the terroir more clearly and reliably—harvest after harvest.

Founder story

Yufan Wu

From first cup to a bridge between origins

Yufan's coffee journey began with an iced Americano. What stayed with him was not only the taste, but the world of curiosity that opened behind it.

Born into a family with more than 25 years of experience in international trade, Yufan spent over eleven years living, studying and working in the United Kingdom from 2013. In 2021, while back in China, he tasted Yunnan specialty coffee for the first time. Its quality challenged the story he had heard about Chinese coffee—and made him wonder why so few people abroad knew what Yunnan could produce.

He began exploring how the best coffees of Yunnan could reach an international audience, with the UK as the first destination. The work demanded more than finding remarkable lots. It required trust at origin, an understanding of export and logistics, and the patience to translate between producers and roasters.

For Yufan, YD Selects is that bridge: a way to give Yunnan coffee the context, care and global conversation it deserves.

Our collaborators

Built through shared work

Producers · estates · researchers · roasters · coffee communities

YD Selects grows through the people who bring different kinds of knowledge to the same table. In Yunnan, producers and estates including Yunlan, Red Dragon, Happenstance and No Stone have shown what patience, varietal diversity and careful processing can reveal.

Roasters in the United Kingdom and Europe help carry those coffees into new conversations. Researchers and processing partners strengthen what can be learned and repeated at origin. Coffee communities in Kenya, Ethiopia and Panama add other traditions and perspectives. Each collaborator remains distinct; together, they expand our understanding of good coffee.

Across origins

Different landscapes.
A shared language.

Since 2025, our view has widened beyond one origin.

We began building closer collaborations between Yunnan and established coffee communities in Kenya, Ethiopia and Panama. Each origin carries its own varieties, processing traditions and ways of reading the land.

When producers exchange methods directly, knowledge moves in both directions. A Panamanian processing idea may open a new possibility for a Yunnan cherry. A Yunnan experiment may offer another origin a different way to think about fermentation, consistency or market access.

02

Exchange without imitation

Learning should protect difference.

This exchange sits at the heart of our growing work. Coffee gives people from distant landscapes a common language. Through a shared table, a cupping bowl or a harvest experiment, they can compare experience without asking one origin to imitate another.

The purpose is to protect difference, learn from it and create relationships that last beyond a single lot. The result is a unique set of YD Selects coffees—each with its own voice, together representing our evolving understanding of good coffee.
