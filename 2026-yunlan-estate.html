Becoming a coffee producer.

A new identity, a shared estate and a deeper commitment to the future of Yunnan coffee.

YD SelectsYunlan Estate · Yunnan, China2026

The move

From trading coffee to sharing responsibility at origin.

In 2026, YD Selects became a co-owner of Yunlan Estate. It is one of the most important moves in our journey: a transition from green coffee trader to coffee producer.

We have always believed that supporting origin requires more than finding exceptional lots. It means understanding the realities behind them - natural conditions, harvest decisions, quality control, processing, logistics and transportation - and choosing to face those challenges alongside producers.

Co-ownership brings us closer to the heart of where our coffee begins. It gives us a deeper responsibility for the land, the people and the long-term quality of the estate. It also changes the way we learn. Decisions are no longer observed from a distance; they are made together, with their consequences shared.

Returning value to origin

Over the preceding years, we returned our profits - and more - to origin. Together with Yunlan Estate, that reinvestment helped create a new modern processing factory. The facility is not simply a piece of infrastructure. It represents a shared belief that better tools, careful production and long-term commitment can help Yunnan coffee express its character more consistently.

The factory gives the estate greater capacity to refine processing, improve quality control and turn learning from each harvest into practical improvements for the next. It creates room for experimentation while keeping the identity of the coffee rooted in its own land.

Standing alongside producers

Becoming a producer means taking part in both the possibility and the risk of origin. We will continue to invest our energy, time and resources at Yunlan Estate, working side by side to improve quality, sustainability and production knowledge.

This chapter also belongs to the roasters and customers who trusted YD Selects during our first years. Their support helped make it possible to return value to origin and build something intended to last beyond a single harvest or coffee lot.

Supporting origin means sharing its responsibilities, not only buying from it.

What comes next

Yunlan Estate will remain a place where cultivation, processing, fermentation research and international exchange meet. Our aim is to help the estate produce coffees of distinctive character and dependable quality, while contributing to a stronger future for Yunnan coffee as a whole.

We are becoming closer to the work, closer to the challenges and closer to the people who make every harvest possible. This is only the beginning of our life as a coffee producer.
