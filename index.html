01 / Origin

A story that starts at the source.

YD Selects began with people whose passion for coffee started in the soil, mountain air and communities of Yunnan, China.

We came together around a simple belief: Yunnan coffee deserves to be known on its own terms, through the character of its land and the skill of the people who grow and process it.

Yunnan, China

More than a commercial origin

The land kept speaking. Producers kept listening.

Across Baoshan, Pu'er, Dehong, Menglian, Lincang and other mountain areas, producers planted higher, selected cherries more carefully, revived Typica and explored washed, natural, honey and controlled fermentation methods.

Their patience revealed florals, citrus, tropical fruit, tea-like clarity, warm spice and deep sweetness.

03 / Exchange

One language. Many origins.

Since 2025, we have built closer collaborations between Yunnan and coffee communities in Kenya, Ethiopia and Panama. Each carries its own varieties, traditions and ways of reading the land.

When producers exchange methods directly, knowledge moves in both directions—without asking one origin to imitate another.

Where we are going

Every origin has its own voice. Coffee helps us listen to one another.

Yunnan is where our story began. The future we are building reaches much further.
